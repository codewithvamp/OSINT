# Practical, responsible OSINT workflows

## Verify a flight claim
Compare aircraft identifiers and observation timestamps from multiple public ADS-B sources. Missing aircraft do not imply concealment; coverage and filtering differ.

## Check satellite position vs imagery
Use orbital catalogs for estimated position, and separately verify imagery acquisition date and resolution. Orbital position is not a live camera.

## Verify a public webcam
Confirm it is intentionally public, inspect time-zone and location clues, and avoid retaining identifiable footage without a lawful basis.

## Assess an authorized domain
Start with passive certificate transparency, public DNS, archived pages and public repositories. Active testing requires written scope and authorization.

## Verify an image
Cross-check reverse-image matches, source context, shadows, weather, landmarks and metadata. Metadata may be absent or manipulated.
