# Responsible Use Policy

- Access only resources you are legally and contractually entitled to use.
- Obtain explicit written authorization before active security testing.
- Never bypass authentication, probe private cameras, exploit devices, or attempt credentials without authorization.
- Publicly indexed services and leaked data are **not** invitations to access or redistribute them.
- Avoid stalking, targeting individuals, doxxing, or publishing sensitive locations.
- Minimize personal data collection; follow applicable privacy and data protection laws.
- Respect provider terms, API rate limits, copyright, and redistribution restrictions.
- Do not submit confidential files or indicators to public analysis services without approval.
- Record collection time, source, coverage, uncertainty, and verification steps.
- ADS-B/AIS tracking may be delayed; orbital positions do not imply live satellite imagery.
- Report suspected vulnerabilities through appropriate responsible-disclosure channels.

This is general guidance, not legal advice.
